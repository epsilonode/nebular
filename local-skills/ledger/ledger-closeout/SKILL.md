# Ledger Closeout

Apply this contract after a bounded substantive work session. Routine reads or informational answers do not require ledger churn.

1. Update each touched roadmap item to its honest state.
2. Add exact, sanitized `@evidence` and `@closed` facts for completed work or proof.
3. Preserve unfinished discoveries as `@blocker`, `@gap`, `@unknown`, `@proof_gap`, or `@note`.
4. Promote durable rationale, contracts, research, or reusable proof guidance through `memory-authoring` and link the card with `@memory`.
5. Append a log line only for a meaningful durable outcome, policy correction, unusual incident, or closeout fact.

Do not represent planned behavior as landed behavior. If verification is missing, leave the item partial/open and state the proof gap.
